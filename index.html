Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2024 | Last Updated: November 1, 2024

Application: Excel Viewer | Developer: Nexabytes Studio

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. Excel Viewer is built with an offline-first architecture. All documents, spreadsheets, formulas, and files you open or edit are processed 100% locally on your device and are NEVER transmitted or stored on our servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexabytes Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use the Excel Viewer mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Document Data & Local Processing (Zero Data Collection)

Excel Viewer allows you to open, view, edit, and create spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls, .csv, and other office formats). Regarding your spreadsheet files:

  • Local Storage Only: All spreadsheet rendering, calculations, and modifications are performed completely on your Android device.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not operate any backend server that receives, reads, or stores your personal spreadsheet documents.
  • File Access Permissions: The App only accesses the specific files you explicitly select via the Android system file picker or open via other applications using standard Android intents.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free to use, we display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data to serve relevant advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance in compliance with Google Play Policies. This may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address and general coarse geographical location (country/city level)
  • Device model, operating system version, and mobile network carrier
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and video completion)

4. Advertising ID & User Opt-Out

You can manage or reset your device's Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads
  • Tap Delete Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. Permissions Used by the App

  • INTERNET: Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob and verify ad network connectivity.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the App to verify whether an active network connection exists before requesting ads.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Excel Viewer does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.

7. Security of Your Data

Because your spreadsheet files are never transmitted to external servers by our application, your sensitive financial, business, and personal data remain solely under your control on your physical hardware.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy within the App or on our public distribution channels.
